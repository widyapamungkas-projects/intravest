Bali is small, but traffic is slow. A day that looks short on the map can take twice as long on the road, so we plan each day around one main area and one main activity.

That is how a trip can include a sunrise trek, a quiet morning in Ubud, and time for a proper meal, without any day feeling like a checklist.
